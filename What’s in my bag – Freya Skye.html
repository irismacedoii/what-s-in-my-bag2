What’s in my bag

Freya Skye’s bag: watch, play, and then show us what’s in YOUR bag!

2. Memory game

You will see 15 items. For each one, decide: was it in Freya’s bag? Click your answer only when you are sure. If it is in the bag, an ad will ask you to explain what she uses it for and why she keeps it there. Say your idea out loud or write it in the box.

3. Your turn: present YOUR bag

Now you are the presenter! Introduce yourself and talk about every item in your bag: what it is, what you use it for, and why it is in your bag.
